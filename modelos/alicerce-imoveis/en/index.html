Frequently asked questions

What do I need to apply for a rental?
A government-issued photo ID, proof of income (your last three pay stubs or bank statements if you're self-employed) and your rental history. We run a standard credit and background check. Along with the security deposit, you can add a co-signer or a lease guarantee service — your choice.
Do you charge to tour a property?
No. Showings, home valuations for sellers and help with mortgage pre-approval are all free. For rentals, the only upfront cost is the application fee, which we disclose before you apply.
How much does property management cost?
A percentage of the monthly rent, set in your management agreement and deducted from each payout. Move-in and move-out inspections, rent collection and tenant screening are all included.
Does the property qualify for FHA or VA financing?
It depends on the property — the ones that qualify say so in the listing. We review the paperwork on every home before it goes on the site, so we can answer that on your very first call.
Do you work outside Charlotte?
Only within the Charlotte metro area. Beyond that, we'd rather refer you to a trusted local agent than work with you from a distance.
I didn't find what I'm looking for. Now what?
Part of our inventory is never advertised, especially rentals. Text us the neighborhood, property type and budget you have in mind, and we'll reach out when something that fits comes in.

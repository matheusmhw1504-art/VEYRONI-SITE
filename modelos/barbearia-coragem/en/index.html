Questions from the front counter

The ones we get texted most, answered all at once.

What if I'm running late?

We'll hold your spot for up to 10 minutes. Past that, shoot us a text: if we can fit you in without making the next client wait, we will. If not, we'll reschedule, no hard feelings.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Just reply to the same text thread where your appointment was confirmed, ideally at least two hours ahead. That way the chair goes to someone else.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, ages 12 and under, with a parent or guardian nearby. For a first haircut, late morning is usually the calmest time.

Do you take cards?

Yes: all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash. Tips can go right on the card.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking right out front. On busy Saturdays, the side street usually has spots.

Do I have to pay in advance?

No. You pay at the end of your visit. Only Club Coragem is billed monthly.

About Jardim Vivo

A neighborhood flower shop, not a designer boutique

We started buying straight from local growers because it just made sense — fresher flowers, fewer miles, people we know by name. That became the way we work: we cut, arrange and deliver in the same cycle, with no inventory sitting around.

Every arrangement leaves wrapped in kraft paper, with twine and ribbon you can reuse. Downtown, deliveries go out by bike — a little slower, but done our way.

Before you order

Delivery & frequently asked questions

Delivery area

Free bike delivery downtown and across the Isthmus. Other Madison neighborhoods, plus Middleton, Monona and Fitchburg, get van delivery for a flat fee calculated at checkout.

Same-day delivery

Order by 2:00 PM for same-day delivery. After that, we deliver the next day. For holidays, please order ahead.

Freshness guarantee

If your arrangement doesn’t last as long as promised, we’ll replace it for free — just text us a photo.

How does the flower subscription work?

You choose how often (weekly or every other week), your delivery day and a rough budget. Each cycle we create a brand-new arrangement from what our growers harvested, never repeating the last one. Pause or cancel anytime, right by text.

Can I choose my delivery date?

Yes. Pick your preferred date in the cart before sending your order. For our busiest holidays (Valentine’s Day on February 14 and Mother’s Day in May), we recommend ordering at least 3 days in advance.

How do I pay?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, plus cash in the shop. Build your order here and we’ll text you a secure payment link to complete checkout.

Do you deliver outside downtown?

Yes, by van, with a flat fee based on the area. Text us the address and we’ll confirm timing and cost.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Orders that haven’t gone out yet can be changed or canceled free of charge. Once an order is on its way, our freshness guarantee has you covered.

Do plants come with care instructions?

Yes — every plant gift comes with a short card explaining watering, light and where it’ll be happiest in your home.

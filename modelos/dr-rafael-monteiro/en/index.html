Frequently asked questions

Common questions before your visit.

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

Most PPO plans don’t require a referral, so you can book directly with our office. If you have an HMO plan, please get a referral from your primary care physician before your visit. Not sure? Text us your plan name and our front desk will check for you.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card and, if you have them, any previous dermatology records, your current prescriptions and a list of the products or medications you apply to your skin. This helps Dr. Monteiro build an accurate picture of your history from day one.

How long is an appointment?

A new-patient visit takes about 40 minutes — time for a complete skin evaluation, a review of your medical history and all of your questions. Follow-up visits are usually more focused, around 20 minutes.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We are in-network with most major PPO plans, including Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross, Blue Shield of California, Cigna and UnitedHealthcare, as well as Medicare. Your copay or deductible is due at the time of your visit. Self-pay patients are always welcome, and we accept all major credit cards, HSA and FSA cards.

Can I book by text?

Absolutely. Tap “Request an appointment,” send us your name, your insurance and the reason for your visit, and our team will reply with available times, usually within one business day. Prefer to talk? Call us at (619) 555-0138.

Does Dr. Monteiro perform in-office procedures?

Yes. In addition to diagnostic evaluations, medical dermatology includes in-office procedures such as skin biopsies and removal of benign lesions when appropriate. Whether you need a procedure, and which one, is always decided during your visit after examining the affected area.

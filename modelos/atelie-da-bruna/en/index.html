A small studio, on purpose

Bruma started in 2017 at Helena Brum's dining table, with one simple question: why is it so hard to find pants that fit at the waist without gaping at the hips? The answer became our own patterns, and the patterns became a house in the Pearl District where five women work today, three of them at the sewing machines.

We make a little because that's how you make it right. Each style is cut in a batch of 20 at most. When a batch sells out, the piece is still available made to order: we cut and sew yours.

We use linen, cotton and tropical wool from mills we know by name, and we keep the fiber sheet for every bolt. Ask us where the fabric in your piece came from, and we can tell you.

How a piece comes to life

  1. Design and pattern made here, on our own blocks.
  2. The sample is fitted on customers from size 0 to 16 before it goes to cutting.
  3. We cut a batch of up to 20 pieces of that style.
  4. Lurdes, Carla or Denise sews the whole piece, start to finish.
  5. The label carries the name of the woman who sewed it.

Find your size

How to measure yourself at home

Use a soft sewing tape over thin clothing. The tape should touch your body without squeezing and stay parallel to the floor. If you can, ask someone to help.

Bust
Around the fullest part of your chest, crossing your back at the same height.
Waist
Around the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the belly button.
Hips
Around the widest part, standing with your feet together.

Tops, dresses and blazers: go by your bust. Pants and vests: go by your waist and hips.

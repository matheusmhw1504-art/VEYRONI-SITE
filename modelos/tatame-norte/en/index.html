PROGRAMS

Every class on the schedule lists its level. Not sure where to start? Just text us.

Ages 14+

JIU-JITSU

Ground grappling in a gi. Beginners have their own class, so you can start from zero at your own pace.

Ages 5–12

KIDS JUDO

Learning how to fall safely, respect their partners and burn off energy the right way. Classes built for their age.

YOUR FIRST CLASS

  1. 01

    Text us

    Tell us the program and the day that works best. We’ll point you to the right class for your level.

  2. 02

    Wear workout clothes

    A T-shirt and shorts with no zippers are all you need. For jiu-jitsu, we’ll lend you a gi for your first class.

  3. 03

    Train with the team

    Our coach keeps a close eye on newcomers. After class, you decide if you want to keep going. No pressure.

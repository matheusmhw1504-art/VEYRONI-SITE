About us

Frequently asked questions

How does getting a website with Veyroni work?

It's simple: you message us and tell us about your business, we design a professional, mobile-friendly website, put it online, and keep taking care of it so everything keeps running.

Where are you based?

We're a small studio in Brazil, run by three partners, and we work fully online with small businesses in the US and Canada. Everything happens by message, so you never need to set aside time for a meeting.

Will my site be in English?

Yes. Sites for US and Canadian businesses are written and built in English. The sample sites in our portfolio are in Portuguese because they were made for our local market, but they show the design and features we can build for you.

Does the site work well on phones?

Yes. Every site we build is responsive, so it adapts to phones, tablets and computers.

Do I need a website if I already have Instagram or Facebook?

Social media helps you get noticed, but many people look for local businesses on Google. A website of your own shows up in those searches, builds trust, and puts everything a customer needs (services, hours, location and contact) at an address that belongs to you.

What kinds of businesses do you build sites for?

Small businesses like shops, restaurants, cafés, barbershops, salons, florists, gyms, clinics, real estate agencies and more. Our portfolio has examples from several industries.

When you open the website

The site has no forms, no sign-up and no advertising cookies.

  • Hosting (Vercel): like any website, the server logs technical access data such as IP address, browser and page visited. This keeps the site running and protects it from attacks.
  • Google Analytics: we use it to know how many people visit the site, where they come from and which pages and buttons they use. It sets cookies in your browser and sends Google data such as IP address, device, browser, approximate city and pages viewed. We only look at overall visit numbers, without identifying you. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Google Fonts: the site's typefaces load from Google's servers, which receive your device's IP address to deliver the files.

When you message us

The site's buttons open a conversation with us. From then on we receive your profile name and number and whatever you write, such as your type of business and what you need.

We use this only to reply, prepare a quote and, if you hire us, build and maintain your website. We do not sell or pass your data to other companies. The messaging app has its own privacy policy.

We keep the conversation while the project or contract lasts and delete it when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep something longer (such as invoice records).
